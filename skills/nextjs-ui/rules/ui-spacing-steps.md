## Spacing comes from a small set of steps

**Impact: LOW** (pages lose a shared rhythm and look subtly different)

Between page sections `gap-6`, inside a card or form `gap-4`, between a label and its control `gap-2`, inline items `gap-1`/`gap-2`. Pick from these before reaching for others, so pages share a rhythm.

**How to check:** look for gaps outside `gap-6`/`gap-4`/`gap-2`/`gap-1` between sections, in cards and forms, and between labels and controls.
