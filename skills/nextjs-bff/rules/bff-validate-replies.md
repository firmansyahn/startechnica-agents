## Validate replies against the OpenAPI document

**Impact: CRITICAL**

Replies are checked against the backend's OpenAPI document on the Next server; a mismatch answers 502 naming the operation and field outside production, and is logged (the reply passed through) in production. Types come from the generated `schema.gen.ts`, regenerated by `yarn gen:api` and checked in CI with `--check`; no hand-written reply types.

**How to check:** the CI template of `nextjs-deploy` (`assets/ci/nextjs.gitlab-ci.yml`) runs `yarn gen:api --check` in the typecheck job, and `scripts/scan.sh` flags a `gen:api` script that no CI file runs with `--check`. A proxy test has the backend fake answer a field of the wrong type: outside production the reply is 502 naming the operation and the field; in production the reply passes and one log entry is written. `scripts/scan.sh` also lists hand-written `...Response`, `...Reply` and `...Dto` types outside `schema.gen.ts`, a name heuristic.
