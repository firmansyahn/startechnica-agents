## Personal fields carry autocomplete hints

**Impact: MEDIUM**

`autoComplete="email"`, `"name"`, `"tel"`, `"current-password"`, `"new-password"`, `"one-time-code"` on personal fields; `autoComplete="off"` for record names and codes the browser shouldn't suggest.
