# Sections

Rule files are named `<prefix>-<slug>.md`; the file name without `.md` is the rule ID. Cite a rule by its ID.

| # | Section | Prefix | Impact | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pipeline shape | `ci-` | MEDIUM | Workflow rules, per-app rules and variables, template overrides, manual jobs, triggers, images |

Impact is the default severity of a finding; raise it by one level when the job builds or releases to production.
